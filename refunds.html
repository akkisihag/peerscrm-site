Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

In short: Try PeersCRM free for 30 days, no card required. After that, you can cancel anytime and keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Purchases are billed by our merchant of record, Paddle, and refunds are handled by Paddle in line with this policy, Paddle’s own Refund Policy, and your legal rights — whichever gives you the most protection.

This policy applies to subscriptions to PeersCRM and its optional add-ons (call-center tools and marketing tools), operated by Peers Consultancy (doing business as PeersCRM). All fees are stated and charged in United States Dollars (USD).

Payments through Paddle

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller and merchant of record, Paddle.com Market Ltd and its affiliates (“Paddle”). When you subscribe, you purchase from Paddle, which handles checkout, billing, applicable taxes, and payment-related customer service. Your receipt and renewal emails come from Paddle and include links to manage your subscription, update your payment method, cancel, or request a refund. Purchases are also subject to Paddle’s Buyer Terms and Paddle’s Refund Policy. If this policy and Paddle’s Refund Policy or your statutory rights differ, the highest level of protection applies.

30-day free trial

New customers receive a 30-day free trial with no credit or debit card required. The trial is intended to let you fully evaluate the Services before making any payment. If you do not start a paid subscription, you are never charged.

Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings, via the “Manage subscription” link in any Paddle receipt email, or by emailing support@peerscrm.com. When you cancel:

  • your subscription will not renew for the next billing period;
  • you keep access to paid features until the end of the current paid period; and
  • we process cancellation requests promptly, and in any event within two (2) business days.

Refunds

Because we provide a full 30-day free trial before any payment, subscription fees are generally non-refundable, including fees for partial billing periods, unused time, and unused features or add-ons. However:

  • Statutory rights always apply. Where the consumer-protection laws of your country grant you a withdrawal or refund right (for example, a 14-day withdrawal right for consumers in the EU/UK for eligible purchases), that right applies and overrides this policy.
  • Discretionary refunds. Paddle may issue a refund on a discretionary basis for requests made within 14 days of the transaction date, reviewed case by case under Paddle’s Refund Policy.
  • Billing errors. If you believe you were charged in error, contact us within 30 days of the charge and we will investigate and correct genuine billing mistakes.
  • Defects. If a material defect prevents you from using the Services as described and we cannot resolve it, you may be entitled to a refund under applicable consumer-protection law.

To request a refund, email support@peerscrm.com or contact Paddle directly via the link in your receipt email or at paddle.net. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method. If you receive a refund, access to the paid features ends.

Add-ons

Call-center and marketing tools are optional add-ons purchased in addition to your base subscription. The same cancellation and refund terms above apply to add-ons unless stated otherwise at the point of purchase. Add-ons linked to a subscription end when the subscription ends.
