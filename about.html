About PeersCRM

A warm, trustworthy CRM built for contractors and growing service businesses.

PeersCRM helps contractors and small teams keep everything their company runs on in one place — leads, projects, clients, subcontractors, estimates, and client-ready reports. We built it because the tools most teams rely on are either too complex or scattered across spreadsheets, email, and sticky notes.

What we believe

Software should feel like help, not homework. PeersCRM focuses on the handful of things that actually move a job forward — a clear pipeline, fast follow-ups, honest reporting, and a team that stays on the same page — and does them well, without the bloat.
